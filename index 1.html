How to play 🃏

Insurance Battle is a turn-based question-card challenge designed for insurance agents and trainers.

  • Choose 1–6 players or teams and the number of cards each will receive.
  • Each turn reveals a client scenario, objection, protection or ethics card.
  • Choose one of three responses. Correct answers earn 10 points; an explanation appears after every answer.
  • Optional timer: 30 seconds. Correct answers with at least 15 seconds left earn 5 bonus points.
  • Tap Next Card to move to the following player. Highest total wins; ties are shared wins.
  • At the end, export the results to CSV if you want training records.

Facilitator tip: Pause after each card and ask, “Why does that answer work for the client?”
